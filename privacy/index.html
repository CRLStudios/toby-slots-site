Toby Slots privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Toby Slots does not collect, store or share any personal information. This page explains what that means in practice.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or software development kits. It does not connect to the internet at all.

What is kept on your device

Your progress is saved on your own iPhone so the game can carry on where you left off:

  • your play-money coins and bet,
  • your Level, the photos and backgrounds you have unlocked, and your Daily Treat,
  • your settings, such as sound and music volume,
  • a Round that was still in progress when you closed the game.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Removing it

Deleting the game from your iPhone removes everything it saved. You can also choose START OVER at the bottom of the game's Settings screen, which clears your progress and keeps only your settings.

Play money only

Toby Slots uses play-money coins. Coins cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or cashed out, and the game offers no purchases and no prizes of any kind.

Children

Toby Slots is a simulated gambling game and is rated for adults. It is not directed at children. As it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Links

The Settings screen has a link to this page. Tapping it opens your web browser; the game itself does not load anything from the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@crlstudios.com.
