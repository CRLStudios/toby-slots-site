Toby Slots support

Need help with Toby Slots, or found something that looks wrong? Email support@crlstudios.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have and the version shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen.

Common questions

Can I buy coins or win real money?

No. Toby Slots is play money only. Coins cannot be bought, sold or cashed out, and there are no purchases, prizes or advertising in the game.

I ran out of coins. What now?

You never run out for long. When you cannot afford the smallest bet, Toby tops you up for free, and there is a free Daily Treat each day you play.

How do I see what each picture pays?

Tap PRIZES on the main screen. It shows what every symbol wins at your current bet, how Free Spins, the Treat Hunt and Play Fetch work, and the shapes of the paylines.

How do I turn the sound or music down?

Tap SETTINGS. There are switches and volume sliders for sound and for music. The note button on the main screen turns the music on and off at any time.

I closed the game in the middle of a feature. Did I lose it?

No. Free Spins, a Treat Hunt or a Play Fetch that was still going will be waiting for you when you open the game again.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Open SETTINGS, scroll to the bottom and tap START OVER. The game asks you to confirm, because this clears your coins, Level and photos and cannot be undone. Your settings are kept.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It works anywhere and never connects to the internet. See the privacy policy.
